About the Studio

Design that works as hard as your brand does

Sodman Creation Hub is a one-person graphic design studio built around a simple idea: a brand's visuals should be impossible to ignore, for the right reasons. Over the past five years I've worked with startups, churches, restaurants, event organisers and growing businesses to turn plain ideas into identities people remember.

Every project starts with the same question — what does this brand need to say, and to whom — before a single shape is placed. The result is design that isn't just good-looking, but does its job: winning attention, building trust, and driving action.
